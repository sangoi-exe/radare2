The enum correction must make existing readers and bridge construction consume the same effective target width rather than independently choose defaults. The calling-convention correction must preserve the already distinct source and operational facts; a different precedence policy is not selected. CALLF correction belongs to its existing native specification/patch owner, not a host-side workaround.
